📊 Dashboard Overview

This Customer Shopping Dashboard provides insights into customer purchasing behavior, sales performance, and product trends. It visualizes total purchase amount, quantity sold, shipping charges, and customer demographics.

💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

- Identify top-performing brands and product categories.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns by gender and age.
- Focus on high-performing products to improve sales.
- Optimize shipping costs and customer satisfaction.
- Use interactive filters for deeper analysis.

Recommendation: Improve business strategies by focusing on customer preferences, popular products, and sales trends.
